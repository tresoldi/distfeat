# Part III: Synthesis

<p class="part-title">Putting it all together with case studies</p>

Part III applies the tools and concepts from Parts I and II to two
extended case studies drawn from Romance historical phonology. The
register shifts from tutorial to analytical narrative: each chapter
poses a linguistic question, develops it with `distfeat` code, and
interprets the results.

## Chapters

**[Chapter 8 --- The Romance Consonant Inventory](ch08_romance_inventory.md)**
assembles the full consonant inventories of Italian, Spanish, French,
Portuguese, and Romanian, maps each segment to its feature
representations across the four system families, and examines where the
systems agree and where they diverge. The chapter produces a
consolidated feature table that serves as the dataset for Chapter 9.

**[Chapter 9 --- Modeling Western Romance Lenition](ch09_modeling_lenition.md)**
models the well-known lenition paths of Western Romance---voicing of
intervocalic stops, spirantization, and segment loss---as
distance-minimizing trajectories in feature space. The chapter compares
distance profiles across systems to determine which feature geometry
best captures the phonological intuition that lenition is a reduction
in articulatory effort.

---

*After completing Part III the reader will have worked through a
complete analytical workflow: from raw inventories through feature
extraction, matrix construction, and distance-based modeling of a
real diachronic sound change.*
